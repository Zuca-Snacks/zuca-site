Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This policy describes what Zuca Snacks collects when you join our pre-order waitlist, why we collect it, who else sees it, and what you can require us to do about it. It is written to describe what our systems actually do. If you find something here that does not match your experience, tell us — that is a bug and we will fix it.

Who we are

Zuca Snacks (“Zuca”, “we”) is a food company based in California. We are the business responsible for the personal information described below. You can reach us at privacy@zucasnacks.com for anything in this policy, or letschat@zucasnacks.com for anything else.

What we collect

Everything below is provided by you, in one form, on this website. We do not buy personal information, and we do not obtain it from data brokers. Only your email address and your marketing consent are required. Every other field is optional, and leaving one blank has no effect on your place in line.

Health-related information

If we ask why you are interested in a high-fiber food, some of the answers — digestion, gut health, a doctor's suggestion — say something about your health. We treat that as sensitive personal information.

We store it only if you tick the separate box next to that question. Ticking the general marketing consent box is not enough. If you select an answer but do not tick that box, your selection is discarded on our server and never written down.

We do not use this information to infer anything about you beyond what you told us, we never send it to any analytics service, and we never share it with an advertiser. Under California law you may direct us to limit the use of sensitive personal information; we already limit it to the single purpose of deciding what to tell you about the product.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Under the CCPA and CPRA those terms are broad and cover some arrangements that do not involve money — none of ours do. If that ever changes, we will post a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link before it does, not after.
  • We run no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no tracking cookies on this site. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no tag manager. This is why you are not being asked to dismiss a cookie banner.
  • We do not profile you or make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us their details, email us and we will delete them.

Fonts

This site currently loads its typefaces from Google's font service, which means Google receives your IP address and browser type when the page loads. No information you type is involved. We are moving these fonts onto our own servers to remove that request entirely; this notice will be updated when that ships.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist records: until you ask us to delete them, or 24 months after we last hear from you — whichever comes first.
  • Addresses that bounce twice: deleted within 30 days.
  • Server logs: 30 days, and they contain no email addresses — only a short one-way hash used to trace errors.
  • If you unsubscribe, we keep a one-way hash of your address on a suppression list indefinitely. That is the only way to guarantee we never email you again, and it cannot be reversed back into your address.

Your rights

California residents have the following rights under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA. We extend all of them to everyone who writes to us, regardless of where you live, because operating two standards is how mistakes happen.

  • Know — what we hold about you, where it came from, why we have it, and who we disclosed it to.
  • Access — a copy of it.
  • Delete — have it erased.
  • Correct — fix anything inaccurate.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing. We do neither, so there is nothing to opt out of, but the right exists and we will honor it if that changes.
  • Non-discrimination — exercising any of these changes nothing about your place in line, your pricing, or the service you get.

How to exercise them

Email privacy@zucasnacks.com from the address you signed up with, and say what you want. That is the whole process.

We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and complete the request within 45 days. If we need longer we will tell you why, and take no more than 90 days total.

To verify it is you, we will reply to the address on file and ask you to confirm. We will never ask you for an ID document, an account, or any information we do not already hold — collecting new personal data to process a deletion request would defeat the point, and the law forbids it.

An authorized agent may act for you with written permission; we will still confirm directly with you.

One limitation, stated plainly

Earlier versions of this site saved a copy of your submission in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. We have removed that behavior, but we cannot reach a copy already sitting in your browser — only you can, by clearing your site data. Nothing else has access to it, and it goes no further than the device it is on.

Emails from us

Every email we send carries a working one-click unsubscribe link and our physical mailing address, as US law requires. Unsubscribing takes effect immediately; we do not use the ten-day grace period the law permits. Our subject lines describe what is actually in the email.

If you ever get email from us that lacks an unsubscribe link, or that you did not ask for, please forward it to privacy@zucasnacks.com. We would rather hear about it from you than from a spam filter.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. The waitlist endpoint validates and rate-limits every submission and is not reachable from any origin but this one. Data is encrypted at rest by our storage provider, and access to the list is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfect, and we would rather tell you what we do than promise you what nobody can.

Found a vulnerability? Email privacy@zucasnacks.com. We will not pursue anyone who reports a genuine issue to us in good faith.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date above and email anyone on the waitlist before the change takes effect. We will not quietly broaden what we do with information you already gave us.
